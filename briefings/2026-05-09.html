9. GLOSSARY

Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP): The monthly count of paid workers in all US sectors except farms, private households, and non-profits, published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics on the first Friday of each month. It is the single most-watched indicator of US labour market health; a surprise of even 30,000 jobs versus consensus can move bond and equity markets significantly.

TTF (Title Transfer Facility): The benchmark European natural gas price, set at a virtual hub in the Netherlands. TTF serves as the primary reference rate for wholesale gas contracts across the EU; traders, utilities, and analysts watch it as the equivalent of Brent crude for European energy markets.

Power by the Hour: A type of long-term service agreement, common in aerospace, where an engine manufacturer charges airlines a fixed rate per flying hour rather than per-incident maintenance costs. It transfers cost unpredictability from the airline to the manufacturer (like Rolls-Royce), aligning incentives around engine reliability and generating a predictable, recurring revenue stream.
