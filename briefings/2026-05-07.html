Why now: The oil-driven equity selloff has reset Nvidia's near-term entry point. With the Blackwell ramp confirmed and Corning deal signalling further differentiation, any macro-driven dip in the high-multiple cohort represents a tactical accumulation opportunity for long-horizon investors.

Risks: (1) US export controls on advanced chips to China could clip ~15% of addressable revenue; (2) a steeper-than-expected macro slowdown in the US reduces hyperscaler capex discretion faster than consensus models.

Bear case in one line: If sovereign AI projects stall and hyperscalers pause capex in response to a 2026 recession, Nvidia's 38× forward multiple would compress sharply with limited dividend support.

9. GLOSSARY

Term Plain English
TTF (Title Transfer Facility) The benchmark price for natural gas traded in Europe, set at a Dutch virtual trading hub. When Middle East tensions rise, TTF spikes because traders worry about LNG supply diversions and pipeline disruption. Higher TTF flows through into European electricity bills and factory energy costs within weeks.
VIX (Volatility Index) Often called the "fear gauge," the VIX measures how much volatility options traders expect in the S&P 500 over the next 30 days. A VIX above 20 typically signals elevated anxiety; above 30 indicates genuine stress. Today's jump to 19.42 reflects rising unease about the oil shock without yet crossing into panic territory.
Bear Steepening A yield-curve move where long-term interest rates rise faster than short-term rates, making the curve steeper — but for bad reasons (inflation fears or fiscal worries), not because growth is strong. Today's gilt curve bear-steepened as oil drove up long-run inflation expectations while the Bank of England's near-term path remained anchored.
