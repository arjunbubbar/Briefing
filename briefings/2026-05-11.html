GLOSSARY

TTF (Title Transfer Facility)
The Dutch benchmark price for European natural gas, quoted in €/MWh. It is the main reference for gas contracts across Europe, traded on the ICE Endex exchange in Amsterdam. TTF is highly sensitive to supply disruptions — whether from Russian pipeline curtailments, LNG cargo diversions, or, as today, Persian Gulf shipping restrictions linked to the Iran conflict.
DXY (US Dollar Index)
A geometric weighted average of the US dollar against a basket of six major currencies: euro (57.6%), Japanese yen (13.6%), British pound (11.9%), Canadian dollar (9.1%), Swedish krona (4.2%), and Swiss franc (3.6%). A rising DXY typically pressures commodity prices (which are USD-denominated), emerging market debt, and risk assets. Today's +0.4% DXY move reflects safe-haven dollar demand following the Iran deal collapse.
Gilt
UK government bonds issued by HM Treasury — named for their original gilded-edge certificates. The 10-year gilt yield is the benchmark for UK mortgage pricing and corporate borrowing costs. When gilt yields rise sharply (as they have today, with the 30-year at a 28-year high of 5.78%), it reflects either higher inflation expectations, fiscal concerns, or both — and directly increases government interest payments on new debt.
