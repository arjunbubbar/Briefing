In the US, the 10-year Treasury yield edged up 2bp to 4.62% on Friday as tariff-driven inflation concerns remain unresolved, with the Fed maintaining its patient, data-dependent stance — swaps currently price roughly 30bp of Fed easing by December 2026, implying one cut and approximately 20% probability of a second. 9. GLOSSARY

Term Premium
The extra yield investors demand for locking money in a long-duration bond rather than repeatedly rolling over short-term instruments. A rising term premium pushes long-term yields higher — as seen in US 30-year Treasuries this week — without any change in central bank policy rates, and reflects investor concern about fiscal sustainability, inflation uncertainty, or supply/demand imbalances in the bond market.
TTF (Title Transfer Facility)
The benchmark natural gas trading hub for continental Europe, based in the Netherlands. TTF prices are quoted in €/MWh and serve as the reference rate for most European gas supply contracts, pipeline deliveries, and LNG import agreements. A sharp fall in TTF — as today's −3.48% move illustrates — typically reflects mild weather, ample storage, or strong LNG supply.
Yield Curve
A graph plotting interest rates on government bonds of the same quality but different maturities, from short (e.g. 2-year) to long (e.g. 30-year). A "normal" upward-sloping curve reflects higher compensation for longer lock-up periods. An "inverted" curve — where short rates exceed long rates — has historically been a reliable recession leading indicator; the current US curve is partially inverted between 2-year and 10-year Treasuries, though the inversion has been narrowing in 2026.
