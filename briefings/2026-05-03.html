Bear in one line: If the iPhone upgrade cycle disappoints expectations built around Apple Intelligence, the premium multiple unwinds sharply with limited dividend support.

9. GLOSSARY

ISM PMI (Purchasing Managers' Index)
A monthly survey of manufacturing (or services) executives published by the Institute for Supply Management. Respondents report whether business conditions — new orders, production, employment, inventories — improved, stayed the same, or worsened versus the prior month. A reading above 50 signals expansion; below 50, contraction. It is one of the earliest leading indicators of US economic momentum.
DXY (US Dollar Index)
A geometrically-weighted index of the US dollar against a basket of six major currencies — the euro (57.6% weight), Japanese yen, British pound, Canadian dollar, Swedish krona, and Swiss franc. A rising DXY signals dollar strength, which typically pressures dollar-denominated commodities (gold, oil) and emerging-market assets; a falling DXY (as seen Friday) generally supports risk appetite and commodity prices.
TTF (Title Transfer Facility)
The Dutch wholesale natural gas trading hub and the de facto European benchmark price for natural gas, quoted in euros per megawatt-hour (€/MWh). It became the global headline reference after Russia's 2022 gas supply disruptions exposed European vulnerability. Traders, utilities, and LNG importers use TTF as the primary reference for European gas forward contracts; a falling TTF reduces energy input costs for European industry and weighs on headline inflation.
