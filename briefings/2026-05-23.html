Bear in one line: If the AI capex cycle cools or export restrictions tighten, Nvidia’s 38× forward P/E has no floor.

9. GLOSSARY

Term Premium
The additional yield investors demand to hold a long-dated bond (e.g. a 10-year gilt) rather than continually rolling over short-term debt. It compensates for the uncertainty of future interest rates. Today’s UK 10Y gilt yield at ~4.55% sits about 80bp above Bank Rate (3.75%), implying a positive term premium — investors want extra compensation for locking up money at a time of elevated and uncertain inflation.
Second-Round Effects
When an initial shock (e.g. surging petrol prices from the Iran conflict) pushes up headline CPI, workers then demand higher wages to keep up, businesses pass on those labour costs in prices, and inflation becomes self-sustaining — a second round. This is precisely what the Bank of England is trying to prevent by holding Bank Rate at 3.75% even as growth softens.
OPEC+ Quota
Agreed production targets among OPEC members (led by Saudi Arabia) and allied producers including Russia. By restricting output, the cartel keeps global supply below demand, supporting prices. Compliance with these quotas — currently high — is one reason Brent crude remains above $100/bbl despite demand uncertainty.
