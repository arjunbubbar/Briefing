9. Glossary

Strait of Hormuz
A narrow waterway between Iran and Oman through which approximately 20% of the world's seaborne oil — and a large share of LNG — passes. Any disruption here immediately tightens global energy supply and raises oil and gas prices worldwide.
Term Premium
The extra yield that investors demand for holding a long-dated bond (say, a 10-year gilt) rather than simply rolling short-term debt over the same period. It reflects uncertainty about future inflation, policy and supply. When term premia rise, 10-year yields rise even if central banks leave short rates unchanged — which is a key driver of gilt and Treasury markets today.
DXY (US Dollar Index)
The ICE U.S. Dollar Index, measuring the dollar against a basket of six major currencies: euro (57.6%), Japanese yen (13.6%), pound sterling (11.9%), Canadian dollar (9.1%), Swedish krona (4.2%) and Swiss franc (3.6%). A rising DXY makes dollar-priced commodities (oil, gold, copper) more expensive for non-US buyers, which can dampen demand and weigh on prices.
