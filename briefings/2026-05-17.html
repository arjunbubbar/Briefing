9. GLOSSARY

Stagflation
When an economy simultaneously experiences high inflation and sluggish (or negative) growth — the worst-case scenario for central banks, since raising rates to fight inflation also risks deepening a recession. The 2026 Iran war energy shock has revived 1970s stagflation fears across Western economies.
DXY (US Dollar Index)
A weighted average of the US dollar's value against a basket of six major currencies (euro, Japanese yen, British pound, Canadian dollar, Swedish krona, Swiss franc). A rising DXY typically weighs on dollar-denominated commodities such as oil and gold, and tightens financial conditions for emerging markets.
TTF (Title Transfer Facility)
The virtual trading hub for wholesale natural gas in the Netherlands, used as the European benchmark price for gas futures. TTF prices are a key barometer for European energy costs — elevated TTF directly feeds into household energy bills and industrial input costs across the EU and UK.
